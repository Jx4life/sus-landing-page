Three ways to save

Pick the mode that fits

Every susu wallet works the same underneath — set an amount, set a schedule. How the money moves is up to you.

Solo

Save on your own terms. Set a goal and a schedule, and Susu keeps your progress visible and on track — no one else involved.

Group

Save toward a shared goal with friends or family — a trip, a gift, an emergency fund. Everyone sees the pot grow together.

Rotation (ROSCA)

The classic susu circle. Everyone contributes the same amount each round, and the full pot rotates to one member at a time.

Where the name comes from

A tradition older than banking itself

Susu is a West African name for the rotating savings circle — a practice where a group pools money on a set schedule and takes turns receiving the full amount. Versions of it exist worldwide, under different names, carrying the same idea: a community can be its own safety net.

We didn't invent this. We just built the app it always deserved — one that keeps the trust, and removes the spreadsheet.
